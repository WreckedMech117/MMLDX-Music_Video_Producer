SongPlanner pipeline

Duration is how long a song M3SongPlanner is told to write. Headroom multiplies it into MiniMaxMusic3TextEncode.max_duration — the encoder's latent ceiling, which the song may finish before, not a second target length. 1.0 gives the encoder exactly the song's length and no room to overrun, reproducing what this form sent before the setting existed. 1.5 is the creator's documented rule; their own exported workflows set both inputs equal, and no render yet has been long enough to settle which is right.
