- (override) R-6 SUPERSEDED by measurement. Director reopened the still-frame choice; timings on this machine show a whole 4.5s shot at half-res costs 270ms vs 170ms for one still, a 15s shot 660ms, a 2s xfade window 150-190ms. Preview becomes a looping clip through the real chain, and transitions ARE previewable in v1.
- (decision) Preview encode settings measured: libx264 ultrafast/crf28 at half the export's dimensions. NVENC is SLOWER at these clip lengths (400-530ms) because init overhead dominates; do not use it for preview.
- (decision) Peak Frame requirement dropped: a 24fps loop shows the drive peak several times a second, so a second still is redundant. Replaced by a drive-envelope readout drawn under the preview.
- (event) sendcmd verified working (90ms for 108 timed commands). Windows gotcha recorded: an absolute path's drive-letter colon breaks filter parsing; run ffmpeg with cwd set to the script's directory and pass a relative filename.
