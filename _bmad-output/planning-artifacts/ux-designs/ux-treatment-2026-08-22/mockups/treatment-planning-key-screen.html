Analyse the song first?
Hears the track, times the lyric sheet's [Tag] blocks against it, fills the Sections row, and measures the beat map the timeline uses. One pass, a couple of minutes. You can skip it — nothing below needs it.
Attribution is neutral, not coloured. A surface wash + left rule says “the machine wrote this”. Provenance is quieter than state — a coloured wash would read as running, error or approved.
Your text is the unmarked default. Only the assistant's contribution is marked, and editing a marked range clears its mark — once you have touched it, it is yours.
No seventh accent. Every meaning here reuses an existing one: amber for planning-mode and staleness, because both are honestly cautions.
Stale is amber, never red. A stale proposal is not an error and nothing is broken — the bus depot may well survive the rewrite. It stays acceptable.
The pass shows elapsed time and nothing else. No bar, no percentage, no estimate — this pass has 26× variance and any number would be a lie.
The planning bar sits over the documents, not beside the chat, because the documents are what is at risk. It reuses the wizard guidance-banner shape.
Every offer is inline. The pre-flight remains the application's only modal; declining an offer always proceeds.
One analysis moment. The offer covers structure and the effects beat map together — a Director must not sit through two passes for a distinction they never asked about.
